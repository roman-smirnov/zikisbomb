## Original generated assets

The plank surface, beveled timber rim, moss lawn, and foliage shadow are original runtime art in `src/gardenBench.ts`, using the palette and meadow treatment of Ziki’s Garden.

Studio reflection faces are generated by `tools/make_stage.py` and baked through Babylon into `studio.env`. Mechanical keypad clicks, rejection sounds and the ambient electrical bed are original Web Audio synthesis. The recordings below are separate third-party assets.
